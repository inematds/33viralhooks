How Health & Fitness grabs your attention

Sell results with less sacrifice. The audience wants the body/energy without the pain—the hook delivers the "mistake holding you back," a shortcut ("half the workout"), and an industry myth ("marketed as healthy").

The formula from the PDF — Context Lean → Scroll Stop → Contrarian Snapback — applied to the 33 hooks in this niche.
