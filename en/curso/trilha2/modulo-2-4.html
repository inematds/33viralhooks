How Self-Development & Productivity grabs your attention

Sell control and clarity. The audience feels stuck/scattered—the hook reframes the problem ("it’s not motivation, it’s this") and promises a simple system ("I got 3 hours back every day").

The formula from the PDF — Context Lean → Scroll Stop → Contrarian Snapback — applied to the 33 hooks in this niche.
