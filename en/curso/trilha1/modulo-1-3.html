The formula: Context Lean → Scroll Stop → Contrarian Snapback

The PDF organizes all 165 hooks into a 3-step formula. It’s the framework you apply to create new hooks in any niche.

🧠 The 3 steps

Each step corresponds to a viewer decision.

  • •Context Lean: get straight to it, with as little context as possible. No “hey everyone.”
  • •Scroll Stop: deliver the scene or statement that stops the scroll in the first few frames.
  • •Contrarian Snapback: flip expectations — challenge what the person believes.
StepViewer questionWhat you deliver
Context Lean"is this for me?"the peak, no beating around the bush
Scroll Stop"huh, what is this?"the scene/statement that stops the scroll
Contrarian Snapback"wait, wasn’t it like this?"the twist that keeps you watching until the end
2

Step 1 — Context Lean (cut the context)

Context Lean = "thin on context." Most videos die because they spend the 3 golden seconds introducing themselves. The formula says to start right at the peak.

🆕 New here? “Lean” here means streamlined

No warm-up. The first word is already part of the hook. Think about starting the video in the middle of the most interesting sentence, not "before it."

✓ Lean context (gets to the peak)

  • ✓"AI replaced a $200,000 job…"
  • ✓"I lost $50,000 in a week…"

✗ Bloated context (loses the first 3s)

  • ✗"Hey everyone, how are you? So, today I wanted to talk about…"
  • ✗"Before we start, subscribe to the channel…"
▸ Going deeper: the "cut after sentence 1" test

Record your video and try delete the entire first line. Most of the time, the video gets better—that line was just a warm-up. Repeat until the first line left is interesting on its own. That’s Context Lean in practice.

3

Step 2 — Scroll Stop (stop the scroll)

Scroll Stop it's the moment when the scene or claim stops the scroll. This is where the Visual Hook shines — the unexpected image that interrupts the feed.

📊 What creates a scroll stop

  • •Image with tension (something about to go wrong, a strong contrast).
  • •A statement that seems impossible or dangerous.
  • •A number too specific to make up.
  • •Movement or transformation right in the first frame.
🔬 Visual scroll-stopper

💬 Spoken: "This habit burns more fat than an hour of cardio."

🎬 Visual: clock ticking as body fat % drops

🔤 Text: Faster Than Cardio

The number counting down stops the scroll—the eye locks onto the transformation before the line ends.

💡 The scroll stop happens BEFORE the word

Often, people stop because of the VISUAL and only then hear the line. That’s why the formula treats the visual as the star, not decoration.

4

Step 3 — Contrarian Snapback (flip expectations)

Contrarian Snapback it's the turn: you bring up a common belief and challenge it — "…it's not", "…it's wrong", "stop doing this". It's what turns attention into curiosity.

🆕 New here? “Snapback” = the sudden turn

It's that "wait, what?" moment. The person believed X, you say "it's not X" — and now they need to stick around to understand why. The gap you create is what keeps them watching until the end.

🧠 Contrarian Snapback patterns

null

  • •"Everyone does X… here’s why it’s a trap."
  • •"You think you need X—but you need Y."
  • •"Stop X—do Y."
  • •"They said X… it isn’t."

⚠️ Without the twist, it becomes empty clickbait

The snapback needs to deliver the twist in the content. Promising "this isn't what shocks you" without a real reveal damages your reputation, and retention drops next time.

The 5 niches and their goals

The material covers the 5 most popular niches. Each one sells a different desire—and the hook needs to speak to that desire. This is the bridge to Track 2.

🤖

AI & Technology

Sells: The future and fear of falling behind. Typical hooks: threat, astonishment, forbidden behind-the-scenes details.

💰

Finance & Business

Sells: Shortcut and financial status. Typical hooks: specific number, confession, counterintuitive rule.

💪

Health & Fitness

Sells: Results with less sacrifice. Typical hooks: the mistake holding you back, the shortcut, the industry myth.

🚀

Self-Development

Sells: Control and clarity. Typical hooks: reframe the problem, promise a simple system.

✈️

Travel & Adventure

Sells: Desire and discovery. Typical hooks: secret place, extreme, price shock.

💡 Same formula, different desire

The formula is the same across all 5 niches. What changes is the DESIRE you tap into. Getting the niche's desire right is half the hook.

6

Choosing your niche and adapting the hooks

You don’t need to invent from scratch: take a hook that already works and adapt it to your niche/language. That’s the shortcut Trilha 3 automates with AI.

🧠 How to adapt a hook in 3 steps

null

  • •1. Choose the pattern: fear, contrarian, number, proof, or curiosity.
  • •2. Change the context: keep the structure, change the subject to your niche.
  • •3. Rewrite the 3 layers: spoken line + visual + on-screen text.

Adaptation example (same framework, 3 niches)

# Skeleton: "Most people think X… it’s not."

AI: "Most people think AI is about speed… it isn’t."

Finance: "Most people think wealth comes from working harder. It doesn’t."

Fitness: "Everyone chases six-pack abs… see what you should pursue."
