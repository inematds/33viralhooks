How Finance & Business grabs your attention

Sell a shortcut and status. The audience wants more money with less effort—so the hook delivers a specific number ($10 mil, 5 minutes), a confession ("I lost US$50 mil"), or a counterintuitive rule ("stop saving").

The formula from the PDF — Context Lean → Scroll Stop → Contrarian Snapback — applied to the 33 hooks in this niche.
